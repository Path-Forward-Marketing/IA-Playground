A sandbox for our site nav & footer

Play with the IA. Try adding pages, renaming items, marking things "coming soon," and switching between launch-day and full-vision views. Nothing you do here affects the real site.

  1. Edit below, preview above. The site preview at the top updates as you type and click in the editor.
  2. Mark what's not ready. Click the yellow SOON pill on any nav item, footer link, or CTA to flag it as not-launching-day-1.
  3. Switch views. Use the View dropdown (top of editor) to flip between Launch only / Launch + Coming Soon (grayed) / Full Vision.
  4. Try a preset. The Preset dropdown loads alternate IA structures — Minimal, Enterprise, Product-led, Developer-first — to compare against the current site.
  5. Send your version back. If you land on a structure you like, click Export JSON at the top of the editor and email me the file.

Best on desktop. Use Reset to preset any time to undo edits.
